The `Badge` component renders a small, pill-shaped label that draws attention to a section of a page, such as a category tag, a status flag, or a section eyebrow above a heading. It accepts any content through its default slot. Use the `style` prop when you need custom colors or spacing.

## Variants

Use the `variant` prop to apply one of the predefined visual styles to the `Badge`. Each variant provides a different color scheme, making it easy to highlight different types of content while maintaining a consistent appearance across your application.
